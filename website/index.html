🍗 Chicken Rice(2 Pax)

Ingredients

  • 2 chicken thighs
  • 2 cups rice
  • 3 cloves garlic
  • 1 tablespoon ginger
  • Chicken stock
  • Soy sauce
  • Sesame oil

Instructions

  1. Cook the chicken in water until fully cooked.
  2. Wash the rice and cook it with chicken stock, garlic and ginger.
  3. Slice the cooked chicken into pieces.
  4. Serve the chicken over the fragrant rice.
  5. Add soy sauce and sesame oil to taste.

🍝 Tomato Pasta(2 Pax)

Ingredients

  • 200g pasta
  • 6 tomatoes
  • 2 cloves garlic
  • 1 tablespoon olive oil
  • ½ cup tomato sauce
  • Salt and pepper
  • Parmesan cheese

Instructions

  1. Boil the pasta until it is cooked.
  2. Heat olive oil in a pan and cook the garlic.
  3. Add tomatoes and tomato sauce.
  4. Season with salt and pepper.
  5. Add the cooked pasta to the sauce.
  6. Top with Parmesan cheese and serve.

🍜 Laksa(2 Pax)

Ingredients

  • 200g rice noodles
  • 2 tablespoons laksa paste
  • 1 can coconut milk
  • 2 cups chicken or vegetable stock
  • 100g chicken or prawns
  • Bean sprouts
  • Boiled egg

Instructions

  1. Cook the rice noodles according to the package instructions.
  2. Heat the laksa paste in a pot.
  3. Add the stock and coconut milk.
  4. Add chicken or prawns and cook thoroughly.
  5. Place the noodles into a bowl.
  6. Pour the laksa soup over the noodles.
  7. Top with bean sprouts and a boiled egg.

🍚 Fried Rice(2 Pax)

Ingredients

  • 2 cups cooked rice
  • 2 eggs
  • 1 carrot
  • ½ cup peas
  • 2 cloves garlic
  • 2 tablespoons soy sauce
  • Cooking oil

Instructions

  1. Heat oil in a large pan.
  2. Cook the garlic and carrots.
  3. Add the eggs and scramble them.
  4. Add the cooked rice and peas.
  5. Pour in the soy sauce.
  6. Stir-fry everything for a few minutes.
  7. Serve hot and enjoy!

🥩 Japanese Beef Rice Bowl(2 Pax)

Ingredients

  • 250g thinly sliced beef
  • 2 cups cooked Japanese rice
  • 1 onion, thinly sliced
  • 2 tablespoons soy sauce
  • 1 tablespoon mirin
  • 1 tablespoon sugar
  • ½ cup water
  • 1 teaspoon cooking oil
  • 2 spring onions
  • Sesame seeds
  • Optional: soft-boiled egg

Instructions

  1. Cook the Japanese rice and keep it warm.
  2. Heat cooking oil in a pan over medium heat.
  3. Add the sliced onion and cook until soft.
  4. Add the beef and cook until it is no longer pink.
  5. Add soy sauce, mirin, sugar and water.
  6. Simmer for about 5 minutes until the sauce becomes slightly thick.
  7. Place the warm rice into a bowl.
  8. Spoon the beef and onions over the rice.
  9. Top with spring onions and sesame seeds.
  10. Add a soft-boiled egg if desired.
  11. Serve hot and enjoy!
