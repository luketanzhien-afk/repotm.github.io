pasta alla vodka

this rich and creamy Pasta alla Vodka relies on a vibrant pink sauce made by emulsifying tomato paste, vodka, and heavy cream.
Ready in about 25 minutes, it is easily tailored to your preferences or dietary restrictions.

Ingredients:

lb short tubular pasta (such as penne or rigatoni)
3 tbsp unsalted butter1 tbsp olive oil1 small yellow onion or shallot,
finely diced3 cloves garlic, minced½ tsp crushed red pepper flakes (adjust to taste)6 oz (about 2/3 cup)
high-quality tomato paste½ cup
vodka1 cup
heavy cream1 cup
reserved starchy pasta cooking waterSalt and freshly ground black pepper (to taste)
Freshly grated Parmigiano-Reggiano and fresh basil leaves

cooking instructions


Cook the Pasta: Bring a large pot of salted water to a boil.
Add the pasta and cook until it is 1 minute shy of al dente.
Reserve 1 cup of pasta cooking water, then drain the pasta.
Sauté the Aromatics: In a large skillet or Dutch oven, heat the olive oil and 1 tablespoon of butter over medium heat.
Add the diced onion/shallot and cook until translucent (about 3-4 minutes).
Add the garlic and red pepper flakes, cooking until fragrant (about 1 minute).
Toast the Tomato Paste: Stir in the tomato paste.
Cook, stirring frequently, until the paste turns a dark brick-red color and caramelizes.
Add Vodka: Pour in the vodka to deglaze the pan.
Let it simmer and sizzle for about 1-2 minutes until the harsh alcohol smell cooks off.
Add Cream & Season: Lower the heat and stir in the heavy cream until the sauce is smooth. Let it simmer gently for about 3-5 minutes. Season with salt and pepper.
Combine and Emulsify: Add the cooked pasta to the skillet, along with about 1/2 cup of the reserved pasta water.
Toss and stir everything together over low heat until the sauce coats the pasta.
Finish: Turn off the heat. Stir in the remaining 2 tablespoons of cold butter and a handful of grated Parmesan cheese, which will help emulsify the sauce into a glossy, velvety texture.
Serve: Garnish with fresh basil, extra Parmesan, and a pinch of red pepper flakes if desired.
